# EIAAW Solutions

> EIAAW Solutions is an AI engineering and integration firm based in Kuala Lumpur, building production AI systems, agents and SaaS platforms for businesses. It runs four live products — Sales Agent, Ai Ads Agency, Workforce and Social Media Team — that take on repetitive work in sales, paid advertising, HR and payroll, and social media, with a human approval gate before anything externally visible happens. It also designs, builds and integrates custom AI systems and agents. Serves Malaysia and Asia-Pacific.

EIAAW Solutions (legal name EIAAW SOLUTIONS, SSM registration 202603133419, CT0164540-H) is registered in Malaysia. Every engagement starts with an AI Impact Assessment grounded in seven non-negotiable ethics principles: Human Dignity First, Transparency, Fairness, Human Oversight, Privacy & Data, Continuous Learning, and True Partnership.

## Human oversight

Every product places a human approval gate before any externally visible action. Sales Agent drafts outreach for human review before send. Ai Ads Agency requires sign-off on creatives and audit fixes. Workforce routes payroll, claims, asset assignments and ledger postings through role-based approvals. Social Media Team holds any post that fails a compliance check, with the reason shown.

## Ethics framework

1. Human Dignity First — every solution must make work more meaningful, not obsolete.
2. Transparency — no black boxes; we explain how systems work in plain language.
3. Fairness — active, measured testing to reduce algorithmic bias.
4. Human Oversight — AI suggests, drafts, analyses; humans make the final call.
5. Privacy & Data — TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest, row-level tenant isolation; GDPR / CCPA / PDPA-aligned, clear data residency.
6. Continuous Learning — built-in feedback loops to detect drift and measure impact.
7. True Partnership — we collaborate with teams, we don't dictate.
