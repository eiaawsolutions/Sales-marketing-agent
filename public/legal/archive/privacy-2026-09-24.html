1. Who we are

EIAAW AI Sales Agent (the "Service") is operated by EIAAW SOLUTIONS, registered in Malaysia (SSM 202603133419, CT0164540-H), Kuala Lumpur ("we", "us"). This notice is issued under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (as amended in 2024) and is written to also meet the privacy laws of the other Asia-Pacific countries our users come from, including Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand, the Philippines, Vietnam, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, Hong Kong and India.

We are the data controller for account holders and visitors to this site, and a data processor for the prospects and leads our customers add to or generate in the Service. Contact our Data Protection Officer at eiaawsolutions@gmail.com (subject "DPO").

2. Data we collect

2.1 Account holders

Username, email address, a bcrypt hash of your password (never the password itself), display name, role, plan, budget settings and email-verification status. These are required to run your account.

2.2 Session data

Opaque bearer tokens (32-byte random hex) with a 24-hour absolute expiry and a 30-minute idle timeout, plus the last-activity timestamp.

2.3 Leads and prospects you add to the Service

When you add a lead manually, by import, or with our lead-generation tools, we store: name, email, company, title, phone, source, notes, a lead score, a pipeline status and activity history. We process this on your behalf.

2.4 Outreach artefacts

For every email or voice interaction: the message content, delivery status, open/click timestamps and, for voice calls, a transcript preview (capped at 500 characters) plus AI-generated sentiment. Audio is processed by our voice provider under its terms (see §6).

2.5 Enquiries and chat on this site

Enquiry form: name, email and message are required so we can reply; phone and company are optional. Chat assistant: before it answers, we ask for your name, email and phone (company is optional). We store both as an inbound enquiry in our own CRM so our team can reply and follow up, and your chat messages are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate answers. Both forms ask for your explicit consent first. If you don't give the required details we can't reply or start a chat — the rest of the site still works. Please don't share sensitive data (health, financial or identity-document details) in forms or chats.

2.6 Voice agent on this site

"Talk to the agent" opens a call with our voice AI provider (Retell AI). The agent always identifies itself as AI. What you say is processed and transcribed to hold the conversation; starting the call is your consent to this.

2.7 Billing data

Subscriptions and payments are processed by Stripe. We store a Stripe customer ID, plan and invoice history, and when you ticked "I agree to the Terms and privacy notice". We never store card numbers, CVVs or bank credentials.

2.8 Usage metrics

Per-account counters for leads created, campaigns sent, AI actions, voice calls and token-level AI cost (model, tokens, USD cost), used for billing, quota enforcement and reliability.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide the Service — lead management, outreach, voice calling, pipeline automation.
  • To authenticate you and keep sessions secure.
  • To generate AI outputs (lead scoring, copy, qualification summaries, call scripts).
  • To reply to and follow up on enquiries made on this site.
  • To bill you, enforce plan limits and prevent abuse.
  • To send transactional email (password resets, receipts, service notices).
  • Only with your consent: to measure our Facebook and Instagram ads (Meta Pixel).

We do not sell personal data, and we don't share it with anyone for their own marketing. We do not use your content or your leads' data to train AI models. Our AI provider (Anthropic) processes API inputs under terms that, at the effective date of this notice, exclude them from training its models; if that changes we will update this notice. We don't send marketing unless you ask for it, and every marketing message lets you opt out.

4. Legal basis

Under Malaysia's PDPA we process personal data with your consent and as set out in this notice (the Notice and Choice Principle). Where other laws require a stated basis, we rely on: consent (enquiries, chat, voice agent, ad measurement); contract (delivering the Service you subscribed to); legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention); and legal obligation (tax, accounting, lawful requests).

5. AI & automated processing

The Service uses Anthropic's Claude API for lead scores, qualification summaries, outreach content and pipeline analysis. When you trigger an AI action we send the relevant context (the lead or campaign record, your instruction, limited conversation history) to Anthropic under its API terms.

AI outputs are suggestions, not decisions. Emails, sequences, voice scripts and status changes need human approval unless you explicitly switch on automation for a workflow.

Right to human review: where an AI-generated outcome materially affects you, you can ask for a human review. We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you by automated means alone.

8. Data retention

  • Sessions: deleted on expiry, logout or password reset. Password-reset tokens: deleted 1 hour after issue. Voice call links: expire after 24 hours to 7 days.
  • Leads, campaigns, activities, pipeline: kept for the life of your account. Deleting a user removes all records they own.
  • Enquiries and chat sign-ups from this site: up to 24 months after our last contact with you, then deleted — unless you become a customer.
  • Billing records: 7 years, for Malaysian tax and accounting requirements.
  • AI cost logs: kept for the life of your account.

You can ask for full deletion at any time (§10). Deleted data may remain in our hosting provider's backups for a short period until they are overwritten.

9. International transfers

The Service is hosted in Singapore, and some sub-processors are in the United States, so your data is processed outside Malaysia and outside your own country. We use providers bound by data-protection terms that protect your data to a standard comparable to Malaysian law, and where your local law requires consent for such transfers, our forms ask for it.

10. Your rights & regulators

  • See the personal data we hold about you and get a copy, including in a portable, machine-readable format.
  • Correct it, or ask us to delete it.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, object to or ask us to limit processing, and stop us contacting you. Withdrawing doesn't affect what we did before.
  • Complain to your data protection regulator.

Email eiaawsolutions@gmail.com with "Personal data request" in the subject. We'll verify it's you, then respond within 21 days, or sooner where your local law requires. There is no charge unless the law allows one for repeated requests, and we'll tell you first.

Regulators: Malaysia — Personal Data Protection Department (JPDP); Singapore — Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC); Indonesia — Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs (Komdigi); Thailand — Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC); Philippines — National Privacy Commission (NPC); Vietnam — Ministry of Public Security (A05); Australia — OAIC; New Zealand — Office of the Privacy Commissioner; Japan — Personal Information Protection Commission; South Korea — PIPC; Hong Kong — PCPD; India — Data Protection Board of India.

If you are a lead contacted by one of our customers through the Service, that customer controls your data — contact them first. If you can't identify them, write to us and we'll pass your request on.

11. Security & breaches

TLS in transit; AES-256-GCM for stored credentials; bcrypt for passwords; opaque bearer tokens with idle timeout; account lockout after repeated failed logins; Helmet-hardened headers including CSP; per-route rate limits. See Security & Compliance. If a data breach is likely to harm you, we will notify the relevant regulator and you as the law requires — in Malaysia, the Personal Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours.

12. Children

The Service is for businesses and isn't directed at anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect children's data; if you think we have, contact us and we'll delete it.

13. Changes

We'll update this page and its effective date when our handling changes. Material changes are emailed to account holders at least 14 days before they take effect, and if a change affects what you consented to, we'll ask again.
