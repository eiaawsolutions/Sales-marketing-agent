Glocomp Systems × EIAAW SalesAgent

Glocomp went exclusive on WEKA AI infrastructure. 107 days ago.

A distributor with 150+ resellers announced an exclusive AI partnership. The demand arrives through the channel — or it does not arrive at all.

This page is a simulated walkthrough of what the EIAAW SalesAgent would have been doing on each of those 107 days — consolidating inbound, scoring channel-aware, routing to the right reseller, and keeping the WEKA funnel warm without adding headcount.

Read this first. Everything about Glocomp on this page is drawn from public sources and cited. Everything about pipeline — leads, scores, contacts, revenue, timings — is invented for illustration. No real person is named as a lead, no contact detail is guessed, and no claim is made about Glocomp’s actual sales performance. The integrity panel lists exactly which is which.

That last row is the whole point. A distributor does not have one sales motion — it has two. It has to recruit and activate the partner who sells, and it has to create end-customer demand that the partner can be handed. Most sales tooling is built for the first motion only, and treats the second as somebody else’s problem.

002

The trigger event, 5 May 2026.

Fig. 02 — Public announcement

WEKA named Glocomp its exclusive end-to-end AI infrastructure partner in Malaysia — WEKA’s NeuralMesh storage and memory system paired with NVIDIA accelerated computing, delivered through Glocomp’s 150+ reseller and SI network, with all infrastructure deployed and managed inside Malaysia.

The announcement names three problems the partnership exists to solve: GPU utilisation, multi-vendor integration, and local data sovereignty. Those three are not marketing copy — they are a qualification rubric. A Malaysian organisation carrying all three is a live opportunity; one carrying none of them is noise.

Joseph Giam, Managing Director at Glocomp, is quoted in the release: “Our customers have been asking for a complete, locally supported AI solution they can deploy with confidence.”

Exclusivity is a clock. It converts to revenue only if demand is captured, qualified and routed to a capable partner faster than the alternatives get evaluated. 107 days have passed.

Glocomp’s inbound arrives in at least five places: the website enquiry form, WEKA co-marketing landing pages, PIKOM and vendor event lists, LinkedIn replies, and partner deal-registration forms. Each is a different inbox owned by a different person.

Day 0 · 11:22 MYT

Handed to the reseller who can actually deliver it.

Day 0 · 11:31 MYT

150+ partners do not have 150 identical capabilities. Routing reads three things: does this partner hold the relevant certifications, do they cover this state, and are they currently active rather than dormant on paper.

The distributor keeps the relationship and the data; the partner gets a lead they can close. That is the difference between a distributor with a CRM and a distributor with a channel engine.

Routing decision
candidate pool → 150 partners
filter cert:ai_infra → 11 remain
filter region:Klang Valley → 6 remain
filter active_90d:true → 3 remain
rank by close-rate on similar deals
→ assigned, SLA clock started (4h to first contact)
2 runners-up held as fallback if SLA breached

The partner and the buyer are not sent the same email.

Day 1–21

Two audiences, two jobs. The reseller needs enablement — margin, demo kit, who to call for pre-sales. The end customer needs proof — sovereignty, utilisation economics, a reference deployment they can believe.

Both sequences pause the moment a human replies. Nothing is more expensive than an automated follow-up landing after a real conversation has started.

The callback at 19:40, answered.

Day 22 · 19:40 MYT

Enterprise buyers call back after hours. The voice agent answers in English or Bahasa Malaysia, confirms who is calling, captures the requirement, and books time with the assigned partner — then writes the transcript and summary into the same record.

It does not quote prices, promise delivery dates, or discuss contract terms. Those are handover triggers, not agent decisions.

005

What makes it Malaysian, not generic.

Fig. 05 — Operating context

PDPA 2010, as amended

Consent captured at the form, purpose recorded with the record, retention enforced on a schedule. Breach-notification and data-protection-officer duties introduced by the 2024 amendments are operational requirements, not a policy PDF.

Data residency

The WEKA release commits to infrastructure deployed and managed inside Malaysia. A CRM that ships lead data offshore contradicts the pitch being made. Hosting region is a sales asset here, not an IT detail.

Public-sector procurement

Government and defence buyers arrive through tender, with MOF registration and ePerolehan conventions attached. The pipeline models a tender clock, not a SaaS trial clock.

Bahasa Malaysia

The voice agent answers in BM or English and switches mid-call. Written follow-up matches the language the buyer opened in. In enterprise and public sector this is table stakes, not a nicety.

Channel conflict rules

Partner-registered deals hold attribution. The distributor never appears to compete with the reseller that sourced the opportunity — encoded as a routing rule rather than trusted to etiquette.

Vendor reporting

Cisco, NetApp, Dell, WEKA and the rest fund activity and expect evidence. Source, campaign and partner stay attached to the record so MDF claims are exportable rather than reconstructed.

Assumptions behind these figures

  • Every number above describes the simulated week on this page. None is a measurement of Glocomp’s real operations, and none is a forecast.
  • Volume of 244 records is chosen to be plausible for a distributor running vendor co-marketing; it is not sourced from Glocomp.
  • The 4-hour SLA is a configurable policy, not a system guarantee. Partner responsiveness is the real constraint.
  • Touch counts assume one qualified opportunity through a 30-day cycle with no procurement delay.
  • A real pilot would replace all of this with measured baselines in the first fortnight — that is the honest way to size it.
